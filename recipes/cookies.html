Sugar Cookies

Description

Just a few ingredients and you wouldn't have to worry about making your next trip to the cafe. You can make these sugar cookies as sweet as you want!

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Leaveners
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Beat sugar, butter, egg, and vanilla in one bowl; mix flour and leaveners in another.
  2. Gradually blend the flour mixture into butter mixture.
  3. Roll the dough into balls and bake until the edges are golden.
